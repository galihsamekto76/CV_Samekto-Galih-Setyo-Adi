DEVELOPMENT OF A 3D BIM OF THE SCHOOL OF APPLIED SCIENCE LIBRARY BUILDING AT UNIVERSITAS GADJAH MADA BASED ON 2019 AND 2024 POINT CLOUDS WITH 4D VISUALIZATION OF THE FIRST FLOOR

his project was completed as my final thesis requirement for graduation, earning an A grade. The project involved the development of a detailed 3D Building Information Model (BIM) of the School of Applied Science Library, using point cloud data from 2019 and 2024. The workflow included data processing using various spatial surveying methods and software, integrating them into a coherent 3D model. Additionally, a 4D visualization of the first floor was created to simulate construction sequencing and temporal changes over time. The project demonstrates proficiency in advanced spatial data analysis, BIM modeling, and visualization techniques, highlighting both technical skill and methodological rigor.
